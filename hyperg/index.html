OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for HyperG

Application: HyperG: Game Booster & FPS HUD
Developer: HyperLabs-Dev
Effective Date: October 21, 2026

Thank you for using HyperG: Game Booster & FPS HUD ("HyperG", "we", "our", or "the app"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how our software operates on your device.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

HyperG is engineered as a local-first gaming optimization and telemetry tool. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or monetize any personal user information.

  • No account registration, email address, phone number, or login credentials are required.
  • No biometric, contact, location, SMS, or camera data is ever accessed.
  • All hardware telemetry calculations (CPU workload, RAM consumption, battery temperature, storage metrics) are computed exclusively on-device in temporary memory.

2. Permissions & Usage Disclosures

HyperG requests only the permissions strictly required to execute its system-level performance enhancements:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to project the floating gaming telemetry HUD overlay (live FPS counter, CPU/GPU meters, clock, and battery stats) while you are playing games. It does not record or intercept screen contents.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Required to maintain background hardware telemetry without Android terminating the monitor during active gaming sessions.
  • Kill Background Processes (KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES): Used by the RAM Booster engine to terminate cached third-party background tasks to free physical memory for gaming.
  • Optional Shizuku Framework: Users may optionally authorize the independent Shizuku system service (uid=2000) to perform privileged system cache compaction and lock the display refresh rate (120Hz/90Hz/60Hz). Shizuku commands run 100% locally on your smartphone and transmit zero data off your hardware.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing software development, HyperG integrates Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements (such as banner ads and occasional game launch interstitials). Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in strict accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HyperG does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children.
